# Prompts Gemini : Boeing (10/11/2026)

Même méthode que « s'enrichir » : 3 décors animés (vidéo 9:16) et 8 accessoires en image fixe sur fond vert.
On réutilise theatre_couleur, l'enseigne, le ruban et le thermomètre de l'épisode du 9/11.

## Décors (vidéo, format vertical 9:16, 8 s)

Common block (to paste at the end of each décor prompt):
"Style: 1930s rubber hose cartoon background, hand-painted gouache, Fleischer and early Disney Technicolor palette (warm yellows, teal, coral red, cream), soft film grain. Vertical 9:16 framing. No characters, no airplanes, no text, no logos. Very slow, continuous camera movement, no cuts, no flicker. Keep the lower third calm and uncluttered (a character will stand there)."

1. decor_ciel : "A wide open cartoon sky seen from high altitude, big rounded cumulus clouds slowly drifting from right to left, a warm afternoon sun in the upper corner, a faint patchwork of fields far below."
2. decor_hangar : "Inside a grand 1930s aircraft hangar with a curved steel roof and tall riveted windows, golden light beams falling on a polished concrete floor, hanging industrial lamps gently swaying, workbenches and ladders along the walls, the empty center left free."
3. decor_piste : "An airport runway at dusk next to a small art deco control tower, runway lights glowing softly, a windsock gently moving, orange and violet sky, the tarmac in the foreground empty and wide."

## Accessoires (image fixe, fond vert uni #00FF00)

Common block (to paste at the end of each prop prompt):
"1930s rubber hose cartoon style, Technicolor palette, thick dark brown outlines, soft painted shading and a small glossy highlight. Single object, centered, fully visible with generous margins, no shadow on the ground, isolated on a flat pure green background (#00FF00), no text, no logos."

1. avion : "A friendly retro-futuristic twin-engine jet airliner in side view facing right, cream and teal fuselage with a red stripe, round portholes, no airline name or logo."
2. porte : "A curved rectangular fuselage panel from an airliner with one small round window and four empty bolt holes at the corners, tumbling slightly in the air."
3. boulon : "A single shiny steel bolt with a hexagonal head, slightly tilted."
4. trophee : "A tall golden trophy cup with two handles on a wooden base with a blank brass plate, sparkling."
5. chaines : "A heavy iron chain loop wrapped around itself with a big closed brass padlock in the middle."
6. fleche : "A big thick red arrow made of glossy enamel, bending and diving steeply downward to the bottom right, cartoon style."
7. commandes : "A tall wobbly stack of cream order forms and paper contracts tied with red string, a few sheets flying off the top."
8. ruban_noir : "A simple black mourning ribbon loop, elegant and sober, satin texture."
