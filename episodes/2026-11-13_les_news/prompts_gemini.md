# Prompts Gemini : Les News de la semaine (13/11/2026)

7 fonds en image fixe, un par diapo (format vertical 9:16 ; je recadre en 4:5 pour Instagram, donc garder l'essentiel au centre).
Les diapos portent des panneaux de verre (texte et chiffres) au centre et Mister Coin en bas : le décor doit rester lisible mais calme au centre.

Common block (paste at the end of each prompt):
"Style: 1930s rubber hose cartoon background, hand-painted gouache, Fleischer and early Disney Technicolor palette (warm yellows, teal, coral red, cream), soft film grain. Vertical 9:16 framing. No characters, no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand names. Keep the center of the image soft and uncluttered (glass panels with text will be placed there) and the lower fifth calm (a character will stand there). Main details at the top and on the sides."
